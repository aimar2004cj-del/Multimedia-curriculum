1. Project Analysis: User Profile

For the development of this website, the following target User Persona has been defined:

Name: Rigby "Hamboning" Raccoon.

Profile: An unconventional, highly energetic park operative and high school graduate who values doing the minimum amount of tedious labor while maintaining a unique sense of personal style.
Needs and Expectations: Rigby requires a modern, streamlined single-page presentation showcase that displays his chaotic work history and unorthodox skill set in a surprisingly readable and professional format. The primary goal is to demonstrate his real-world experience—ranging from high-stakes golf cart operation to resolving cosmic crises—without resorting to long, overly formal paragraphs that would bore the reader.


2. Project Analysis: Information Architecture

The information architecture of this project is structured as an elegant single-page layout that delivers all key details without requiring unnecessary clicks or subpages. At the very top, the header section immediately introduces Rigby alongside his official title as a park worker and his profile image, establishing his core brand identity right away.

Following the header, the main body is organized vertically into distinct semantic sections separated by clean dividers. It begins with a brief biography summarizing his chaotic work ethic, followed by a chronological timeline of his employment history at the Municipal Park and the local arcade. Below experience, his educational journey at Park Academy is highlighted. The layout then transitions into a flexible tag grid showcasing his core skills (such as extreme golf cart driving and rock-paper-scissors expertise), ultimately concluding with language proficiencies and document pagination in the footer.

3. Project Analysis: Visual Design

The visual design combines a clean editorial layout with vibrant accent colors that fit Rigby's energetic personality. The color palette features a neutral slate background offset by a crisp white central container with an electric blue primary bar across the top, giving the overall document a polished, modern appeal.

Typography relies on a clean, highly legible sans-serif font family with a clear visual hierarchy. Section headings are highlighted in vivid blue, while job titles and primary roles utilize a vibrant orange accent to draw immediate focus to key achievements. Finally, his skills are presented using pill-shaped tags with soft background fills, transforming his unique talents into a contemporary portfolio component.
